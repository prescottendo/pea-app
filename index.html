Add Huddle to My iPhone

Then, in about a minute

  1. Tap Allow if your iPhone asks to download a configuration profile, then Close.
  2. Open the Settings app and tap Profile Downloaded near the top.
  3. Tap Install (top right), enter your iPhone passcode, then tap Install again and Done.
  4. Go to your Home Screen. The Huddle icon is there. Tap it and sign in with your @prescottendo.com Google account.

Your iPhone will label it "Unsigned." That's expected: it only adds the Huddle icon to your Home Screen and changes nothing else. To remove it later, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

Don't see "Profile Downloaded" in Settings? Make sure you opened this page in Safari, then tap the button again. If your iPhone saved the file to Downloads instead, open the Files app, tap Downloads, tap Huddle.mobileconfig, then continue with step 2.

Already have the old P.E.A. icon? Install this once to rename it Huddle; your iPhone replaces the old one.
